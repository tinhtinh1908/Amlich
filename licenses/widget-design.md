# Native widget redesign

Design references (layout principles; no third-party widget code copied):
- https://developer.android.com/design/ui/widget
- https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/appwidgets/layouts
- https://www.samsung.com/ru/support/mobile-devices/calendar-widgets-and-countdown-on-samsung-galaxy/

All three existing providers remain compatible with installed widgets.
Day widgets use a single neutral card with prominent solar day and secondary
lunar/calendar information. Month widget uses a balanced header, native calendar
cells, five or six week rows in a 3x3 launcher footprint, without a title/control bar or bottom summary.
App light/dark preferences and all five palettes apply to widget text, highlight
and surfaces. Month photo backgrounds and notes/holidays remain supported.

Only RemoteViews-supported layouts/views are used. Background bitmaps are capped
at 480 pixels and text remains native/accessibility-readable. Month cell highlights
share one 60px bitmap. Clicks open the corresponding date using existing date intents.
Resizing rebuilds the layout; XML weights and date auto-sizing prevent six-row
overlap at the minimum 180x190dp size. Day widgets compact month labels at narrow
sizes. Provider metadata declares target launcher cells and safe minimum sizes.

Preview images are illustrative drawings generated by tools/generate_widget_previews.py,
not Android launcher screenshots. Compiled resources and Java/signature checks pass;
physical launcher placement, theme changes and resizing require device verification.

The narrow vertical date uses a width-limited font size accounting for system font scale, avoiding the old two-digit ellipsis.

All three widgets share the chosen photo background. Date-widget images crop to their own aspect ratio and use a stronger dark overlay with white primary text; removing/replacing a photo refreshes all providers immediately.

## 2x2 tear-calendar widget
A fourth provider displays a large solar date, full weekday, lunar date, day stem/branch, auspicious guardian and holiday/note/year detail. It uses the same photo rendering, updates, app palette and date-opening intent as the compact date widgets. Minimum 140x160dp and target 2x2 cells preserve readable geometry.

## Corrected 2x2 footprint
The tear widget uses 110x110dp legacy minimums and targetCellWidth/Height=2 with resizeMode=none, replacing the 160dp height that forced a third row on HyperOS. Its distinct paper layout has a binding band, two punched holes, oversized condensed solar date, weekday and lunar footer; extra rows were removed. Existing placed widgets retain their old allocation until re-added.

## Expanded 2x2 information layout
The fixed 2x2 paper now has separate solar and lunar columns, a weekday, day/year stem-branch line, two-column guardian/solar-term detail and holiday/note/month-stem footer. Its smaller-size fallback hides only the lunar caption to keep the lunar date visible. Target cells and legacy minimums remain unchanged.
